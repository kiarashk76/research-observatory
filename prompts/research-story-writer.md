# Research story: fixed study table

Read prompts/report-workflow.md for the current standard. Research story is a platform-generated table, not a living paper. Each row is one study: title, brief purpose, paper-section PDF link and detailed-report PDF link.

The supervisor updates the row via update_study (title, question, paper_pdf, detailed_pdf). Do not write notes/research-story.md directly. Do not produce narrative sections, embedded figures, a synthesis, revision essays, story drafts or Codex editorial reviews. Do not regenerate PDFs just to update a row. Keep missing PDFs visibly unavailable. Existing reports and historical narrative archives remain preserved.

Keep one current row per study, with a Status column. Update the same row and replace its links when reports change; add a row only for a new study. Do not display older versions. Set execution for study progress and status_note for a concise explanation of report work, pending review or a blocker. Be accurate: queued or paused writing is not active writing. Historical files are not automatically deleted.
